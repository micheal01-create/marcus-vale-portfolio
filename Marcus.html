About Marcus

Turning moments into visual stories. I’m Marcus Vale, a 22-year-old freelance photographer and creative designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. What started as a simple interest in photography at the age of 17 gradually grew into a passion for capturing the world through my own perspective. Over the years, photography has become more than just taking pictures for me — it has become a way of documenting moments, expressing creativity, and telling stories without the need for words. Living in Lagos has given me endless inspiration. The city is full of movement, personality, culture, and contrasts, and I enjoy finding those details in places people might normally overlook. Whether it’s a quiet moment on a busy street, the energy of a fashion shoot, the atmosphere of an event, or the beauty of a new destination, I’m always looking for the story behind the image. My work focuses mainly on street, fashion, and travel photography. I enjoy experimenting with different perspectives, lighting, compositions, and environments to create photographs that feel authentic and memorable. Every location and every person brings something different to the frame, and that’s what keeps photography exciting for me. As a creative designer, I also understand that strong visuals are about more than just looking good. They should communicate something. That’s why I work with brands, individuals, models, and creative teams to create visual content that represents who they are and what they stand for. My goal is simple: to help people and brands tell their stories through powerful visuals. Whether it’s building a personal brand, documenting an important event, creating content for a fashion campaign, or simply capturing a moment worth remembering, I want every photograph I create to have a purpose. I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and exploring new ways to improve my craft. With every project, I aim to create work that feels personal, authentic, and visually striking — while continuing to grow as both a photographer and a creative designer.
